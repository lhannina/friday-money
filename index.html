New here? How this works (30 seconds)
  1. Every Friday one of our paychecks hits the shared account. They alternate: one week Person A, next week Person B.
  2. Pick the Friday at the top. Type what actually came in.
  3. Check off each bill as you pay it. The amounts come out of that week's money.
  4. Follow the What to do next box. It changes as you go: how much to leave for next Friday, and and what to pay the everyday card.
  5. Everything saves for both of us. Once a month, update balances on the Goals tab.
